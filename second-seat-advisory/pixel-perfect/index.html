Installation
  1. Click Copy Signature to Email.
  2. Open your Gmail/Outlook signature settings and paste into a new signature.
  3. Send a test message to check all three clickable contact rows.

This version uses exact image slices to preserve the artwork. Remote pictures may be hidden until a recipient allows images. The text within pictures is not editable.
